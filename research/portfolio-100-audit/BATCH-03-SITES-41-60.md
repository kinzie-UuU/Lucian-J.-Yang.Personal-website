## Batch 03 Early Patterns

### 1. Studio confidence can come from proposition, not just spectacle

Portal One is important here:

- strong proposition
- repeated framing
- clear booking path
- less dependence on complex visual tricks

This reinforces the idea that premium feeling can come from rhetorical structure.

### 2. Production-company sites often stay useful because the work is already cinematic

Robot shows that:

- if the media itself is strong
- the site can stay structurally straightforward
- and still feel bold

This is relevant for Kinzie whenever project proof becomes strong enough to carry more weight.

### 3. Hybrid designer-developer portfolios are useful, but dangerous to over-borrow

Karim Saab, Fine Thought, Jordan Delcros, and Clément Grellier show different intensities of hybrid identity.

For Kinzie, the lesson is:

- borrow execution quality
- borrow interaction discipline
- do not borrow the self-positioning

### 4. Strong references for Kinzie in this batch

Most useful:

- Portal One for structure and persuasion
- Robot for personality-with-clarity
- selective ideas from Karim Saab for solo capability framing

Useful only as boundary references:

- Jordan Delcros
- Phantom Studios
- Fine Thought
