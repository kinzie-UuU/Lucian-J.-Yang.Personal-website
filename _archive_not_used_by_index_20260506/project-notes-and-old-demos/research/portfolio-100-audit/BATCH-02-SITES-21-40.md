## Batch 02 Early Patterns

### 1. The stronger sites often separate "identity energy" from "commercial legibility"

Chipsa, Double Play, and Ragged Edge all show different ways to do this:

- Chipsa: high visual energy, but strong service structure
- Double Play: strong concept metaphor, but clear offer and contact
- Ragged Edge: almost no spectacle needed because proposition and results are strong enough

### 2. Not all high-design sites need aggressive interaction

Jens Bosman and Ragged Edge are especially important here:

- Jens proves calm image-led sequencing can still feel premium
- Ragged Edge proves boldness can come from point of view and evidence, not from interface fireworks

### 3. Concept systems are strongest when they control more than aesthetics

Double Play is a good example:

- the tennis metaphor is not only visual
- it informs copy
- it informs process
- it informs CTA

This makes it more reusable as a skill pattern than a merely "cool-looking" site

### 4. Kinzie should keep favoring authority-through-judgment

The best references in this batch for Kinzie are:

- Ragged Edge for strategic authority
- Jens Bosman for restraint
- Double Play for concept coherence
- Chipsa for expressive structure under discipline

The least useful for Kinzie remain:

- heavy creative-technologist identity sites
- opaque app-shell entries
- studios whose value is mostly immersive 3D or entertainment-tech bravado
