# Batch 01 Audit

Scope: sites 1-20 from Muzli's portfolio list  
Status: first-pass audit  
Method: direct observation where possible, supplemented by Muzli descriptions  
Important: this is not the final taxonomy. It is a baseline record set.

## Confidence Key

- `high`
  Direct site inspection produced enough structural evidence
- `medium`
  Site plus Muzli description gave a workable read, but not a full one
- `low`
  Mostly dependent on Muzli description or limited crawl output

## Batch 01 Early Patterns

### 1. The first screen is usually either a thesis or a filter

Across the first 20, the homepage tends to open with one of two moves:

- a strong identity thesis
- a works filter or category split

Very few of them begin like a conventional brochure page.

### 2. Works are often embedded into the homepage, not postponed

The stronger sites do not hide their best proof behind deep navigation.

They use:

- continuous project rails
- featured work strips
- category-led work partitions
- archive-like index blocks

### 3. Personality and clarity are not opposites

Brand Appart, General Condition, Cappen, and Sergey Lisovskiy all show different balances of personality and clarity.

This matters for future skill design:

- we should not treat "clear" as "boring"
- we should not treat "expressive" as "unclear"

### 4. Studio sites split into at least three families

- commercial-polished studios
  Brand Appart, Cappen
- personality-forward studios
  General Condition, Working Stiff
- immersive / advanced-tech studios
  Alche, Orage, T-KO

### 5. Kinzie should borrow structure more often than surface

The early evidence suggests Kinzie is more likely to benefit from:

- Brand Appart's conversion-aware structure
- Sergey Lisovskiy's archive credibility
- Twice's restraint
- selected pieces of Cappen's project sequencing

Kinzie is less likely to benefit from directly borrowing:

- immersive metaverse logic
- loud comedy branding
- heavy creative-developer self-display
