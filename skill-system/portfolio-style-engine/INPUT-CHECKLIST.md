# Input Checklist

Use this as the minimum intake sheet for the portfolio-style engine.

## Identity

- What kind of designer / creator / studio is this?
- Is this a solo practice, a team, or a studio?

## Goal

- Is the site for leads, hiring, authority, personal expression, or all of the above?

## Audience

- Who should understand the site fastest?
- clients
- recruiters
- peers
- collaborators

## Content

- How many strong projects exist?
- Are there full case studies?
- Are the images strong enough to carry large sections?

## Taste

- What should the site feel like?
- What should it never feel like?

## Interaction

- Is strong interaction actually desired?
- Should motion stay subtle?

## Constraints

- preferred stack
- WebGL allowed or not
- performance priority
