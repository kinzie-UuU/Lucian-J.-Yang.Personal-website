## Batch 04 Early Patterns

### 1. The list is increasingly splitting into repeatable business archetypes

By this point, repeated families are clear:

- product/system designer portfolios
- startup-facing performance studios
- production / post-production companies
- creative-developer portfolios
- premium calm digital agencies

This is useful because the later taxonomy will not need 100 unique classes.

### 2. "Performance + design" is becoming a recurring agency signal

Basement Studio, SoScale, and Bulletproof all, in different ways, tie design to outcomes.

This matters for Kinzie because your site should also eventually say:

- not just "beautiful"
- but "effective under real constraints"

### 3. Calm structure is still one of the strongest premium signals

Lazy Eight is especially important here as a repeated confirmation of a pattern already seen earlier:

- strong grid logic
- content clarity
- subtle motion
- high trust without loudness

### 4. Kinzie's strongest matches in this batch

Most useful:

- Bulletproof for strategic confidence
- Lazy Eight for premium restraint
- RAYRAYlab for one-person studio clarity
- basement.studio for design-plus-performance framing
- OddCommon for focused independent-studio identity

Least useful:

- heavy creative-developer identity sites
- futuristic interface-first studios
- media categories where image/motion naturally carries more weight than structural logic
