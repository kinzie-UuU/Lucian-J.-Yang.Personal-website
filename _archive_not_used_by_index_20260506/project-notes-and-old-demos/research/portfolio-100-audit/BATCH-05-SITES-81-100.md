## Batch 05 Early Patterns

### 1. The last fifth confirms the major families rather than inventing new ones

By site 100, the recurrent portfolio families are now unmistakable:

- global strategy / design companies
- refined independent studios
- typography- or motion-led personal portfolios
- immersive tech-first studios
- niche specialist studios with strong category clarity

### 2. Typography and proposition can carry premium feeling without heavy interaction

Burocratik, Your Creative, and parts of November point toward a strong skill family:

- strategic voice
- typographic confidence
- system-led memorability

### 3. Boundary cases are just as useful as positive references

Immersive Garden, Utsubo, dverso, Chain Labs, and Merodev are valuable not because Kinzie should resemble them, but because they help define where Kinzie should stop.

### 4. Strong Kinzie-aligned references from this batch

Most useful:

- Burocratik for typographic brand confidence
- Your Creative for value-led clarity
- selective elements of November for intellectual design-practice tone
- selective elements of Mat Voyce for formal consistency

Useful mostly as a boundary:

- Merodev
- Immersive Garden
- Utsubo
- dverso
- Chain Labs
